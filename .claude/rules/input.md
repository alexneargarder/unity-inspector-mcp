# simulate_input

- **Forms**: exactly one of `action`, `binding`, `sequence` or `tracks`; a step takes `action` or `binding`, or neither for a pause (`{wait: N}`).
- **The plan limit**: 60 seconds, totalling a hold's `duration`, `count` presses with their `duration` and `interval` (an `action`'s or a `binding`'s alike), or a `sequence`'s or a track's holds and waits, and the mod also refuses a plan the frame floors below would push past 60s at the game's measured frame rate.
- **Frame by frame**: the mod runs the plan on the main thread: a press is held for its `duration` and at least one whole frame, a key pressed again is released for a whole frame first, and the edge reads (`GetControllerPressing*`, `IsControllerPressing*`) report a press on its first frame only, as a real button does, while `GetInput`, the menu reads and `GetControllerHolding*` report it on every frame it is held. Each patched read mirrors the original's own gates (`InputReader.IsBlocked`, a cutscene, players held still, the delay after an unpause).
- **Replies**: the mod answers `simulate_input` at once with `pending`, `planId` and `remainingMs`, and `sendCommand` polls `input_plan` until the plan's result comes back, so no request holds the connection while a plan runs and every form replies when its plan ends; the mod keeps only the 32 newest unread results, so a plan whose caller gave up polling is dropped once newer ones finish.
- **`framesRead`** (a number for one press, else one entry per press or step, null for a pause) counts the frames on which a patched read returned the press; a press no frame read fails the call with the reasons, and one read while no bro could act on it (no level, a pause, no live bro) carries a `warning`, judged as the press is first read, so a `fire` or `jump` the pause menu takes as its accept carries it though the accept closes the pause. A bro key read at `timeScale` 0 with nothing else in the way carries a `note` instead, `Time.timeScale is 0: the bro took the press, but nothing moves until the clock runs`, since the bro's `Update` still runs and takes the press, its state and velocities set, and only motion waits for the clock.
- **Takeover and stops**: a plan for a player that uses any key of that player's running plan takes over, and the running plan fails naming the keys taken over; plans of other players, or on other keys, run side by side. A scene load, or the mod being disabled or unloaded, stops every plan, queued ones included, and a closed connection, `restart_server`'s included, stops its plans with no reply, as `cancel_input_plan` does the one plan it names, for the connection that started it (see Cancellation in `connection.md`). A stopped plan fails, naming why and the press it cut short or never began, unless every press had already been held its full duration, when only a trailing wait was cut and it answers success with `endedEarly`; a press whose own first frame requested the loading scene (a pause-menu Restart or Return to Menu confirm loads synchronously) counts as held in full.
- **`tracks`**: 2 to 8 sequences, all started on the same frame, a track's leading pause step being its offset. No two tracks may press the same key, an action name matched case-insensitively. The call is one plan with one result, whose `tracks` holds each track's own result and whose `error`, when a track failed, reads `track <n>: <its error>` for the first failed one.
- **Which controller reads it**: only `player`'s controller reads the input; with no local player playing it comes from controller 4, which joins the next free slot, and the reply's `note` says so. On a join screen (`JoinScreen` or `HeroSelectController`), while a local player plays, a press for a slot nobody plays comes from the lowest gamepad controller (4-7) no joined slot owns and joins the next free slot, the `note` naming it; in a level it reads no frame. A press for a slot a player on another machine plays reaches no gameplay and joins nobody, and still drives a menu. `start` and `escape` from a keyboard player come from the first gamepad controller (4-7) no local player owns, and fail when every one is owned.
- **`start`, `escape` and the pause**: `escape` is the cancel press and the menus' decline, and never opens the pause menu; screens that read the real Escape key only (`Lobby`, `KeyboardInput`) never see it, and `HeroSelectController` takes it only from a joined controller. The pause menu a controller's `start` opened takes that controller's input alone: its `start`, `escape` or accept on Resume closes it, while another controller's press and the real Esc key do nothing to it. Keyboard players share one controller, the first gamepad controller no local player owns, so one keyboard player's `escape` closes a pause another's `start` opened.
- **Actions**: `up`, `down`, `left` and `right` move and aim the bro; `fire` shoots the primary weapon; `jump` held with `down` drops through a platform; `sprint` runs; `gesture` plays the taunt; `start` opens the pause menu; `escape` backs out of menus. `highFive` throws back a live grenade within 20 units, and the switch it activates, pickups and item boxes included, is the first registered `MobileSwitch` in range, a rescued prisoner being the one it high-fives.
- **Refused** while a `capture_animations` sweep runs, as `run_test_script` is. Malformed plans (an unknown step key, a step with both `action` and `binding`, a negative value, a number that is not a whole number, `count` below 1, `duration`/`count`/`interval` beside a sequence or `tracks`, a pause step with a `duration`, malformed `tracks`) are refused, by the server and again by the mod
- **`binding`** presses a mod's RocketLib key binding, named `<mod id>/<binding name>` as `AllModKeyBindings` keys it, for `player`; a `sequence` step takes `binding` in place of `action`. The mod splits the name after the longest registered mod id it starts with, since a binding name may hold a `/` (`Utility Mod/Pause/Unpause Game`), and matches the mod id and the binding name case-insensitively when no exact one matches. Its postfix on `KeyBinding.IsDown` answers true for that player's entry of the binding while the press is held, so `PressedDown` and `Released` follow from RocketLib's own code, while `GetAxis`, which never calls `IsDown`, does not see the press; this holds whatever key the binding has or whether it has one: Furibrosa calls `IsDown` on its `Switch Weapon` binding every frame, so an unbound one is pressed too. A mod has to call `IsDown` for the press to land: Utility Mod polls only the bindings with a key assigned, and only player 0's, so an unbound one, or another player's, is never read and the press fails with no frame read, naming whether the binding has a key for that player. Takeover, `count`/`interval`, release on a cancel or a closed connection and the scene-load stop work as for an action; a binding press carries no bro `warning` and no clock `note`. An unknown binding is refused, listing the bindings that mod registers, or the mods with bindings when the mod is unknown; with RocketLib absent or the patch not installed, every binding press is refused naming why
